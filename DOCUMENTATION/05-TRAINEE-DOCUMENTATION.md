## Getting Started

### What is BMDC 1.1?

BMDC 1.1 is an online training management system that helps you:
- Register for training programs
- Mark your daily attendance
- Track your progress
- Download your certificates
- Access training materials

### Before You Start

You'll need:
- ✅ Email address (for login)
- ✅ Phone number (for contact)
- ✅ Basic computer or smartphone (to access the system)
- ✅ Internet connection
- ✅ Camera on phone or computer (for attendance photos)

### Getting Help

- **During Registration**: Look for the **[?]** help icon on each page
- **After Login**: Contact your training coordinator or local admin
- **Forgotten Password**: Use **[Forgot Password]** link on login page
- **Technical Issues**: Contact the system administrator

### Step 3: Submit Registration

1. Review all information on final page
   - Check spelling of name
   - Verify email address correct
   - Confirm phone number
   - Check all details accurate

2. Click **[Submit Registration]**
   - Registration submitted
   - Success message: "Registration received!"
   - Instructions shown: "Wait for admin approval"

3. **What happens next**:
   - Your registration sent to local admin
   - Admin will review your information
   - You'll receive email when approved or rejected
   - If approved: Login credentials sent in email

---

### Step 4: Approval by Admin

**Waiting for approval**:
- ⏳ Usually 1-3 business days
- 📧 Check your email for approval notification
- ⚠️ Check spam folder if email not received

**If Approved**:
- Email arrives with subject: "Your Registration Approved"
- Email contains:
  - Your login credentials (email/username)
  - Temporary password
  - Link to login
  - Instructions for first login

**If Rejected**:
- Email arrives with subject: "Your Registration - Action Required"
- Email contains:
  - Reason for rejection
  - Instructions for reapplication
  - How to correct issues
- Can apply again after corrections

## Attendance Marking

**URL**: `/trainee/attendance`

### Understanding Attendance

**Why Mark Attendance?**
- Tracks your participation in training
- Proof that you attended
- Required for certificate (usually 80%+ minimum)
- Helps coordinators manage programs

**When to Mark?**
- Morning session: Usually 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  - Mark after you arrive and before you leave
- Afternoon session: Usually 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  - Mark after you arrive and before you leave

**With What?**
- Camera on phone or computer
- Internet connection
- Takes about 2 minutes per session

---

### Understanding Attendance Status

| Status | What It Means | What Happens |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **Present** | You marked both morning and afternoon | Counted as 1 day present |
| **Partial** | You only marked one session | May be counted as 0.5 day |
| **Absent** | You didn't mark attendance | Counted as day absent |
| **Excused** | You had valid reason (sick, etc.) | Not counted against you |
| **Holiday** | No attendance required that day | Doesn't affect your rate |

### Viewing Your Attendance Record

1. From **Attendance** page, select program
2. Calendar shows your attendance history
3. See each day marked with color
4. **Click day** to see details:
   - Photo you submitted
   - Times in/out
   - Status

### What If You're Sick or Have Emergency?

**If you can't attend**:

1. **Contact training coordinator** as soon as possible
   - Email or phone
   - Explain your situation

2. **Provide evidence** (if required):
   - Medical certificate (for illness)
   - Documentation of emergency
   - Explanation letter

3. **Coordinator will mark as "Excused"**
   - Won't count against your attendance
   - You can make up the day (if offered)

4. **Do NOT just skip** without notifying!
   - Counts as absent
   - Affects your attendance percentage
   - May affect certificate eligibility

## Common Tasks

### Task 1: Complete Your First Day of Training

**Goal**: Successfully attend first training day with attendance marked

**Steps**:

1. **Morning** (before 12:00 PM):
   - Go to **Attendance** page
   - Select today's date
   - Click **[Mark Morning Attendance]**
   - Take clear selfie
   - Submit attendance

2. **Afternoon** (after 1:00 PM):
   - Go back to **Attendance** page
   - Click **[Mark Afternoon Attendance]**
   - Take afternoon selfie
   - Submit attendance

3. **Verify**:
   - Check attendance calendar
   - See green ✓ for today
   - Click day to see your photos

✅ **Result**: First day of training complete with attendance recorded

---

### Task 2: Monitor Your Attendance Progress

**Goal**: Track how well you're attending and ensure you stay above 80%

**Steps**:

1. **Open Dashboard**
   - See your attendance percentage
   - See programs you're enrolled in

2. **Go to Attendance**
   - Select program
   - View calendar
   - See color-coded attendance history

3. **Analyze**:
   - Count green days (present)
   - Count red days (absent)
   - Calculate percentage
   - Compare to 80% requirement

4. **If concerned**:
   - Missing many days?
   - Attendance dropping?
   - Contact training coordinator
   - Explain situation
   - Ask about makeup sessions

5. **Plan improvements**:
   - Set alarm for early morning
   - Arrange transportation
   - Plan for recurring conflicts
   - Ask about flexible scheduling

✅ **Result**: Awareness of attendance status and proactive planning

---

### Task 3: Download Your Certificate

**Goal**: Get official proof of training completion

**Steps**:

1. **Wait for completion**:
   - Program end date must pass
   - You must have 80%+ attendance
   - Training coordinator generates certificates

2. **Check email**:
   - Receives email: "Your certificate is ready"
   - Link to download in email

3. **Go to Certificates**:
   - Navigate to **My Certificates**
   - See your newly issued certificate

4. **Download PDF**:
   - Click **[Download PDF]**
   - Save to computer
   - File named: `Certificate-[ProgramName]-[Date].pdf`

5. **Print**:
   - Open PDF

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Can't log in

**Possible causes**:
- Wrong email/username
- Wrong password
- Account not activated
- Account locked (multiple failed attempts)

**Solutions**:
1. Check email address or username spelling
2. Verify caps lock off
3. Try resetting password:
   - Click **[Forgot Password]** on login page
   - Enter email
   - Follow reset instructions
   - Set new password
   - Try logging in
4. If still failing, contact administrator

### Issue: Can't mark attendance

**Possible causes**:
- Program not yet started (start date in future)
- Program already ended
- Outside marking window
- Technical issue with camera

**Solutions**:
1. Check program start date (must be today or earlier)
2. Check program end date (must be today or later)
3. Check time (must be during program hours)
4. Try different browser
5. Check camera permissions allowed
6. Try on mobile phone instead
7. Contact training coordinator

### Issue: Attendance not saving

**Possible causes**:
- Internet connection dropped
- Browser/app crashed
- File upload failed
- Device issue

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection (test on other website)
2. Refresh page and try again
3. Try from different device
4. Try different browser
5. Clear browser cache
6. Restart device
7. Contact support if issue persists

### Issue: Certificate not appearing

**Possible causes**:
- Program not yet completed
- Attendance below 80%
- System hasn't generated yet
- Browser cache issue

**Solutions**:
1. Check program end date (must have passed)
2. Check attendance percentage:
   - Go to Attendance page
   - Calculate percentage (days present / total days)
   - Must be 80% or more
3. Refresh page or clear browser cache
4. Log out and log back in
5. Contact training coordinator to verify

### Issue: Photo not uploading

**Possible causes**:
- File too large (max 5 MB)
- Internet connection slow
- Browser issue
- Camera access denied

**Solutions**:
1. Check file size (compress if needed)
2. Check internet connection
3. Try different browser
4. Grant camera permission:
   - Browser settings
   - System privacy settings
5. Try on mobile phone
6. Contact technical support

### Issue: Forgot username/password

**Solutions**:

1. **If forgot username**:
   - Go to **[Forgot Username]** link
   - Enter your email
   - System sends your username

2. **If forgot password**:
   - Go to **[Forgot Password]** link
   - Enter your email
   - Click reset link in email
   - Set new password
   - Log in with new password

3. **If can't access email**:
   - Contact training coordinator
   - Provide ID verification
   - They can send you temporary access

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: What's the minimum attendance to get a certificate?**  
A: 80% (or as stated by your program). Some programs may require higher.

**Q: What if I'm sick?**  
A: Contact your training coordinator immediately. Bring medical certificate if possible.

**Q: Can I mark attendance for previous days?**  
A: Usually no, only for current/today. Contact coordinator for exceptions.

**Q: How long are certificates valid?**  
A: Certificates are permanent records. They don't expire.

**Q: Can I share my login with a friend?**  
A: No. Each person needs their own account. Your friend must register separately.

**Q: What if I fail (below 80% attendance)?**  
A: You won't get certificate for that program. Some organizations offer retake options.

**Q: Can I enroll in multiple programs?**  
A: Yes, if available. Ask your training coordinator.

**Q: How do I print my certificate?**  
A: Download PDF and print on paper. Contact coordinator if you need official copies.
